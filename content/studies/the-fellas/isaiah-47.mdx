## Overview

Isaiah 47 is a prophetic lament over Babylon — a nation that trusted in its own power, wealth, and wisdom rather than in the living God.

## Scripture Reading

> "Come down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter Babylon; sit on the ground without a throne, daughter of the Babylonians."
> — Isaiah 47:1

## Key Themes

### 1. The Pride of Self-Sufficiency

Babylon said in her heart: "I am, and there is none besides me."

- How often do we operate from this same posture?
- Where in your life are you trusting in your own strength rather than God's?

### 2. The Danger of Misplaced Security

> "All the counsel you have received has only worn you out!"
> — Isaiah 47:13

### 3. God's Sovereignty Over Nations and Lives

- Nothing catches God off guard.
- His purposes will stand.

## Discussion Questions

- In what areas of your life are you most tempted toward self-reliance?
- What does it practically look like to trust God's sovereignty?

## Application

Identify one area where you have been relying on your own wisdom or strength. Bring it before God in prayer.

## Prayer

Lord, forgive us for acting as though we are sovereign over our own lives. Teach us to build our security on You alone. Amen.
