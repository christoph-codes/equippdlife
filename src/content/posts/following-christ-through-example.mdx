# Following Christ Through Example  
**Date:** December 20, 2025 

**Scripture Reading:** 1 Corinthians 11:1

> *“Follow my example, as I follow the example of Christ.”*

Paul makes a bold statement here, but it is not rooted in pride. It is rooted in discipleship. He understands that following Christ is not only taught through words but demonstrated through lives. His invitation is not to imitate him for his own sake, but to follow him as he follows Christ.

This verse reminds us that faith is visible. Our walk with Jesus shapes how we speak, how we treat others, how we respond to pressure, and how we live when no one is watching. Paul knew that people were watching his life, and he embraced that responsibility with humility and intention. His goal was not personal recognition, but faithful representation of Christ.

This call also challenges us personally. Every believer influences someone. Whether we realize it or not, others are learning what it looks like to follow Jesus by observing our lives. Discipleship is not about perfection; it’s about direction. When our lives are pointed toward Christ, our example becomes an invitation for others to follow Him too.

## Main Takeaways  
- Discipleship is modeled through how we live, not just what we say.  
- Following Christ shapes our actions, attitudes, and priorities.  
- Every believer influences others through their example, whether intentionally or not.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Philippians 3:17:** “Join together in following my example…”  
- **1 Peter 5:3:** “Be examples to the flock.”  
- **John 13:15:** “I have set you an example that you should do as I have done.”  

Examine your walk today and ask, “Does my life point others toward Christ?” Commit to following Jesus faithfully, trusting that as you walk with Him, your example can lead others closer to Him as well.
