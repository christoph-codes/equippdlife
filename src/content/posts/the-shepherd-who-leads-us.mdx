# The Shepherd Who Leads Us  
**Date:** December 4, 2025 

**Scripture Reading:** Psalm 23:1-6

> *“The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing.  
> He makes me lie down in green pastures,  
> He leads me beside quiet waters,  
> He refreshes my soul.  
> He guides me along the right paths  
> for His name’s sake.  
> Even though I walk  
> through the darkest valley,  
> I will fear no evil,  
> for You are with me;  
> Your rod and Your staff,  
> they comfort me.  
> You prepare a table before me  
> in the presence of my enemies.  
> You anoint my head with oil;  
> my cup overflows.  
> Surely Your goodness and love will follow me  
> all the days of my life,  
> and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.”*

Psalm 23 is one of the most beloved passages in all of Scripture because it reveals the heart of God as our Shepherd. David writes from a place of deep trust, knowing that when the Lord leads him, he lacks nothing. God not only provides what we need — He leads us to rest, restores our souls, guides us on the right path, and stays with us through every season.

The imagery is rich and intentional. Green pastures speak of provision and peace. Still waters reflect calm and renewal. Right paths point to God’s direction and wisdom. Even the darkest valleys — places of fear, uncertainty, and suffering — are transformed by His presence. We are never alone. His rod protects, His staff guides, and His presence comforts.

This Psalm also shifts from talking **about** God to talking **to** God. In the valley, David says, “You are with me.” The Shepherd becomes personal. Intimate. Near. It is in the hardest places that we often become most aware of God’s presence.

The Psalm ends with confidence. God prepares a table for us even in the face of opposition. He anoints us with honor and blessing. His goodness and love do not chase us occasionally — they follow us constantly. And the promise of dwelling with Him forever gives us unshakable hope.

## Main Takeaways  
- God is our Shepherd who provides, leads, restores, and protects.  
- His presence is our greatest comfort, especially in dark valleys.  
- His goodness and love pursue us daily, and His eternal promise gives us hope.
