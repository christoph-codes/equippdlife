# One Father, One Family  
**Date:** February 19, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Malachi 2:10

> “Are we not all children of the same Father? Are we not all created by the same God? Then why do we betray each other, violating the covenant of our ancestors?”

Malachi confronts a serious issue among God’s people: broken faithfulness toward one another. He begins by reminding them of something foundational. They share one Father. They were created by the same God. This truth should shape how they relate, speak, and live within community.

When we forget that we belong to one spiritual family, division grows easily. Pride, betrayal, and selfishness fracture relationships. Malachi points out that faithfulness to God cannot be separated from faithfulness toward others. How we treat one another reflects how seriously we take our shared identity in Him.

This verse also highlights accountability. God’s people were called to represent His character. When they acted unjustly toward one another, they misrepresented Him. The reminder of one Father re-centers their responsibility. Unity is not uniformity, but it is rooted in shared belonging.

As we move through this season, Malachi 2:10 invites us to consider how we reflect our Father’s heart. Do our actions strengthen unity or weaken it? Remembering that we share one Creator changes how we approach forgiveness, honesty, and loyalty.

## Main Takeaways  
- Shared identity in God shapes how we treat one another.  
- Faithfulness to God includes faithfulness in relationships.  
- Unity grows when we remember we belong to one spiritual family.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Ephesians 4:4-6:** “One body and one Spirit… one God and Father of all.”  
- **John 13:35:** “By this everyone will know that you are My disciples…”  
- **Colossians 3:13-14:** “Forgive as the Lord forgave you… put on love.”  

Honor your Father today by strengthening unity wherever you can. Choose faithfulness in your relationships and reflect the character of the One who created and calls you His own.
