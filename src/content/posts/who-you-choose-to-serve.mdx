# Who You Choose to Serve  
**Date:** March 10, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Romans 6:16

> “Don’t you realize that you become the slave of whatever you choose to obey? You can be a slave to sin, which leads to death, or you can choose to obey God, which leads to righteous living.”

Paul presents a clear and sobering truth: obedience always leads somewhere. No one lives completely independent of influence. Every decision we make reflects what we are ultimately choosing to follow. Allegiance is not only expressed through words, but through consistent patterns of living.

Romans 6 reminds us that freedom in Christ does not remove responsibility. Instead, it invites us to intentionally choose righteousness. When a person repeatedly submits to sin, it slowly shapes their direction and character. In contrast, when someone submits their life to God, obedience produces growth, transformation, and life.

This verse encourages honest reflection. The habits we cultivate, the voices we listen to, and the choices we repeat all reveal where our loyalty truly rests. Faith is not only what we believe privately; it is what we practice daily.

As we move through today, Romans 6:16 reminds us that our lives are always moving toward something. The question is not whether we are serving, but whom we are serving. True freedom is found when our obedience aligns with the One who gives life.

## Main Takeaways  
- Obedience reveals where our true allegiance lies.  
- Repeated choices shape the direction of our lives.  
- True freedom is found in submitting to God’s righteousness.

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Joshua 24:15:** “Choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve…”  
- **John 8:34-36:** “Everyone who sins is a slave to sin… but if the Son sets you free…”  
- **Galatians 5:16:** “Walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh.”

Choose your obedience intentionally today. Align your decisions with God’s truth and allow your daily actions to reflect your commitment to the One who leads you toward life.
