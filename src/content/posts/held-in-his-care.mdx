# Held in His Care  
**Date:** January 20, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Psalm 66:9

> “He keeps us alive  and does not allow our feet to slip.”

The psalmist points to a quiet but powerful truth: our lives are sustained by God. Every step forward, every breath taken, and every moment preserved is evidence of His care. Life does not rest on our ability to maintain control, but on God’s faithfulness to hold us steady.

This verse speaks especially to seasons when stability feels fragile. There are moments when progress seems slow or footing feels uncertain, yet God remains actively involved. He does not merely observe our journey; He preserves it. Even when we stumble, He prevents our steps from slipping beyond His reach.

God’s sustaining power often works beneath the surface. We may not always recognize His protection in real time, but looking back reveals His hand guiding, guarding, and carrying us through. The fact that we are still standing is itself a testimony to His grace.

As we move forward through this season, Psalm 66:9 invites us to trust the God who holds our lives together. When uncertainty rises, we can rest in the assurance that our steps are known to Him and our lives are secure in His care.

## Main Takeaways  
- God actively sustains and preserves our lives.  
- Stability comes from God’s faithfulness, not our control.  
- Even unseen protection is evidence of God’s care.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Psalm 121:3:** “He will not let your foot slip…”  
- **Proverbs 3:23:** “Then you will go on your way in safety…”  
- **Jude 1:24:** “To Him who is able to keep you from stumbling…”  

Trust God with each step today. Release the need to control what lies ahead and rest in the confidence that your life is held securely by the One who faithfully preserves and guides you.
