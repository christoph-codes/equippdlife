# Time Alone with the Father  
**Date:** March 7, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Mark 1:35

> “Before daybreak the next morning, Jesus got up and went out to an isolated place to pray.”

Mark records a simple but powerful moment in the life of Jesus. After a full day of ministry, healing, and serving others, Jesus rose early in the morning and went to a quiet place to pray. Despite the demands placed upon Him, He prioritized time alone with the Father.

This verse reveals the importance of spiritual rhythm. If anyone could have justified skipping quiet time, it would have been Jesus. Yet He modeled dependence by regularly withdrawing to pray. Time in prayer was not an interruption to His work; it was the foundation that sustained it.

Mark 1:35 also reminds us that solitude creates space for clarity. Life becomes crowded with responsibilities, noise, and expectations. Without intentional moments with God, it becomes easy to drift into self-reliance. Prayer re-centers the heart and realigns our priorities with His will.

As we move through today, this verse invites us to consider how we are making space for God. Busyness can easily crowd out the very relationship that gives our lives direction and strength. Following Christ means learning from His example.

## Main Takeaways  
- Even Jesus prioritized time alone with the Father.  
- Prayer strengthens and sustains spiritual life.  
- Quiet time with God brings clarity and alignment.

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Luke 5:16:** “Jesus often withdrew to lonely places and prayed.”  
- **Psalm 5:3:** “In the morning, Lord, you hear my voice…”  
- **Matthew 6:6:** “Go into your room, close the door and pray…”

Make time to step away and seek God today. In the quiet moments with Him, you will find the strength, wisdom, and peace needed for everything that lies ahead.
