# From Faith to Faith  
**Date:** February 26, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Romans 1:17

> “This Good News tells us how God makes us right in his sight. This is accomplished from start to finish by faith. As the Scriptures say, “It is through faith that a righteous person has life.””

Paul anchors the gospel in a powerful truth: righteousness is revealed through faith. It does not begin with human effort or spiritual achievement. It begins with trust. From the moment of salvation to the daily walk of obedience, faith remains central.

This verse reminds us that the Christian life is not sustained by striving, but by dependence. Faith is not a one-time decision. It is an ongoing posture. Each day requires renewed trust in God’s character, promises, and provision. Growth happens when belief moves from theory into lived reliance.

Romans 1:17 also highlights the continuity of faith. The phrase “from faith to faith” reflects progression. Faith deepens as we experience God’s faithfulness repeatedly. The more we trust Him, the more clearly His righteousness is revealed in our lives.

As we move through this season, this verse invites reflection on where your trust is anchored. Are you attempting to carry burdens alone, or are you walking by faith daily? Righteousness is not achieved; it is received and lived out through trust in Christ.

## Main Takeaways  
- Righteousness begins and continues through faith.  
- The Christian life is sustained by daily dependence on God.  
- Faith grows stronger through continued trust in God’s faithfulness.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Habakkuk 2:4:** “The righteous will live by faith.”  
- **Hebrews 11:6:** “Without faith it is impossible to please God.”  
- **Galatians 2:20:** “The life I now live in the body, I live by faith…”  

Choose to walk by faith today. Trust God in the unseen moments, depend on His righteousness rather than your own effort, and allow your life to reflect the steady confidence that comes from believing Him fully.
