# Plans and Answers  
**Date:** March 3, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Proverbs 16:1

> “We can make our own plans, but the Lord gives the right answer.”

Proverbs presents a tension that every person experiences. We plan, organize, and prepare for what lies ahead. Planning itself is not wrong. In fact, thoughtful preparation reflects responsibility and wisdom. Yet this verse reminds us that the final outcome does not belong solely to human effort.

God remains sovereign over the results. We may prepare our thoughts and intentions, but the direction of events ultimately rests in His hands. This truth humbles us. It reminds us that success is not produced by control alone, but by alignment with God’s will.

Proverbs 16:1 also invites trust. When outcomes shift from what we expected, it does not mean God has stepped away. Sometimes His answers redirect us toward something better, something unseen in our original plans. Trust grows when we recognize that God’s wisdom reaches beyond our limited perspective.

As we move through today, this verse encourages a balance between diligence and surrender. We plan responsibly, but we remain open to God’s guidance. Faith allows us to hold our plans loosely while trusting His direction fully.

## Main Takeaways  
- Planning is wise, but outcomes belong to God.  
- God’s sovereignty humbles human control.  
- Trust grows when we submit our plans to Him.

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Proverbs 16:9:** “In their hearts humans plan their course, but the Lord establishes their steps.”  
- **James 4:13-15:** “You ought to say, ‘If it is the Lord’s will, we will live and do this or that.’”  
- **Psalm 37:5:** “Commit your way to the Lord; trust in Him and He will act.”

Bring your plans before God today. Prepare with wisdom, but trust Him with the outcome, knowing that His guidance will always lead you where you truly need to go.
