# Mutually Encouraged  
**Date:** February 13, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Romans 1:12

> “When we get together, I want to encourage you in your faith, but I also want to be encouraged by yours.”

Paul expresses a desire not only to strengthen the believers in Rome, but to be strengthened alongside them. His words reveal humility and a deep understanding of how God works through community. Faith is not meant to grow in isolation. God often builds His people through shared encouragement.

This verse highlights the beauty of mutual strengthening. Even an apostle recognized his need for fellowship. No one matures alone. When believers gather with sincerity and openness, faith deepens through testimony, prayer, and shared perseverance. Encouragement becomes a two-way gift.

Romans 1:12 also reminds us that encouragement is active. It involves presence, listening, and speaking truth in love. God uses ordinary conversations and faithful relationships to renew hearts and reinforce hope. Community is one of His primary tools for sustaining belief.

As we move through this season, this verse invites us to examine how we engage with others in faith. Are we allowing others to strengthen us, and are we intentionally strengthening them? God designed His people to grow together.

## Main Takeaways  
- Faith grows stronger within community.  
- Encouragement is meant to flow both ways.  
- God uses relationships to build and sustain belief.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Hebrews 10:24-25:** “Let us consider how we may spur one another on…”  
- **Ecclesiastes 4:9-10:** “Two are better than one…”  
- **1 Thessalonians 5:11:** “Encourage one another and build each other up.”  

Reach out intentionally today. Strengthen someone in their faith and allow yourself to be strengthened in return, trusting that God grows His people through shared encouragement and steadfast community.
