# Filled by the Spirit  
**Date:** December 26, 2025 

**Scripture Reading:** Exodus 31:3

> *“And I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, with understanding, with knowledge and with all kinds of skills.”*

This verse reveals something powerful about how God works through people. Before Bezalel ever crafted anything with his hands, God filled him with His Spirit. The work came after the filling. God did not simply give instructions and expect human effort to carry them out; He equipped His servant with divine wisdom and understanding first.

Exodus 31:3 reminds us that God values skill, creativity, and craftsmanship. These are not secondary or secular gifts; they are spiritual gifts empowered by the Spirit of God. Wisdom guides decisions, understanding brings clarity, and knowledge equips us to carry out God’s work faithfully. When God fills someone, it is always for purpose — never by accident.

This truth applies to every believer. God does not call us to tasks He has not prepared us for. Whatever He places in our hands, He also places His Spirit within us. When we rely on Him rather than our own strength, our work becomes an act of worship and our obedience becomes fruitful.

## Main Takeaways  
- God equips His people with His Spirit before assigning the work.  
- Wisdom, understanding, and skill are gifts empowered by God.  
- What God calls you to do, He also prepares you to accomplish.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Zechariah 4:6:** “Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit…”  
- **James 1:5:** “If any of you lacks wisdom, ask God…”  
- **1 Corinthians 12:4-6:** “There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit…”  

Trust that God has filled you for the work He has placed before you. Lean into His Spirit today, knowing that wisdom, understanding, and strength flow from Him as you walk faithfully in what He has called you to do.
