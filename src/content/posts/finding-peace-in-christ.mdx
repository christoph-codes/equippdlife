# Finding Peace in Christ

**Date:** September 5, 2024  
**Scripture Reading:** John 16:33

> “In this world you will have trouble. But take heart! I have overcome the world.”

## Devotion Content

Jesus never promised an easy life. Instead, He promised peace — His peace — in the midst of difficulty. Troubles will come, but His victory remains greater.

Christ’s peace isn’t based on circumstances, but on the truth that He has overcome sin, death, and every force of evil. When fears rise or pressures mount, we anchor ourselves in Him.

## Main Takeaways

- Peace in Christ is deeper than circumstances.
- Jesus has already overcome the world.
- Faith keeps us grounded when life feels unstable.

## Cross-References

- Philippians 4:6-7
- Isaiah 26:3
- Romans 8:38-39

**Closing Thought:**  
Rest in the peace only Jesus can give. Whatever you face, remember His victory is your assurance.
