# Open My Eyes  
**Date:** January 28, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Psalm 119:18

> *“Open my eyes that I may see wonderful things in Your law.”*

This prayer reveals a posture every believer must learn. The psalmist does not assume understanding comes automatically. Instead, he asks God to open his eyes. Scripture is not merely information to be read; it is truth to be revealed. Without God’s help, we can read His Word and still miss its depth and beauty.

Psalm 119:18 reminds us that spiritual sight is a gift. God’s Word contains wonder, wisdom, and life, but it requires humility to receive it. The psalmist approaches God with expectation, believing there is more to see, more to understand, and more to be shaped by. This prayer invites God to move beyond surface reading and into heart transformation.

This verse also teaches us how to approach Scripture daily. Rather than rushing or reading out of habit, we are called to pause and invite God into the process. When our eyes are opened, familiar passages come alive, conviction becomes clear, and guidance becomes personal. God reveals what we need when we seek Him with openness.

As we continue through this season, Psalm 119:18 invites us to slow down and listen. God desires to show us wonderful things, not only about His Word, but about how it applies to our lives right now. Seeing clearly begins with asking humbly.

## Main Takeaways  
- Understanding God’s Word requires spiritual openness.  
- God reveals truth to those who seek Him humbly.  
- Scripture becomes alive when God opens our eyes.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Luke 24:45:** “Then He opened their minds so they could understand the Scriptures.”  
- **James 1:5:** “If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God…”  
- **2 Corinthians 4:6:** “God… made His light shine in our hearts…”  

Begin today by asking God to open your eyes. Approach His Word with humility and expectation, trusting that He will reveal the truth you need and shape your heart through the wonder of His Word.
