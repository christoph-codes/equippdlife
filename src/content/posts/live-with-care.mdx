# Live With Care  
**Date:** January 8, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Ephesians 5:15

> *“Be very careful, then, how you live—not as unwise but as wise.”*

Paul’s instruction is both practical and profound. He calls believers to pay close attention to how they live, recognizing that faith is expressed through daily choices. Wisdom is not merely about knowing the right things, but about living in a way that reflects God’s truth and priorities.

Living carefully does not mean living cautiously out of fear. It means living intentionally. Paul contrasts wisdom with carelessness, reminding us that an unexamined life can drift away from what matters most. When we slow down and consider how our time, words, and actions align with God’s will, wisdom begins to shape our lives.

This verse invites reflection. How we live speaks louder than what we say. Wisdom shows up in how we steward our time, how we treat others, and how we respond to opportunities and challenges. A wise life is one that remains attentive to God’s direction and responsive to His guidance.

As we continue forward in this season, Paul’s words remind us that intentional living honors God. When we choose wisdom over distraction and purpose over impulse, our lives begin to reflect the light of Christ more clearly.

## Main Takeaways  
- Wisdom is revealed through intentional living.  
- Careful living aligns daily choices with God’s priorities.  
- A thoughtful life reflects attentiveness to God’s guidance.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Psalm 90:12:** “Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom.”  
- **Proverbs 4:26:** “Give careful thought to the paths for your feet.”  
- **Colossians 4:5:** “Be wise in the way you act toward outsiders.”  

Choose to live thoughtfully today. Invite God to guide your decisions, shape your habits, and help you walk in wisdom so that your life reflects His truth and purpose.
