# Who We Choose to Obey  
**Date:** February 14, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Romans 6:16

> “Don’t you realize that you become the slave of whatever you choose to obey? You can be a slave to sin, which leads to death, or you can choose to obey God, which leads to righteous living.”

Paul presents a sobering reality: obedience always leads somewhere. No one lives without submitting to something. Whether we realize it or not, our daily decisions reflect what we have chosen to follow. Allegiance is not declared only with words, but with patterns.

Romans 6 reminds us that freedom in Christ is not freedom to drift. It is freedom to choose righteousness. Every repeated decision forms direction. When obedience aligns with sin, it shapes character and consequence. When obedience aligns with God, it produces growth and life.

This verse invites us to examine the subtle rhythms of our lives. What we consistently give ourselves to eventually defines us. Obedience is not just about isolated moments, but about the direction of the heart over time. God’s grace frees us from sin’s authority, but we are still responsible for choosing whom we will follow.

On a day often associated with affection and loyalty, this verse carries timely weight. Love is shown through devotion. In the same way, our love for God is revealed through obedience. The heart ultimately follows what it trusts and values most.

## Main Takeaways  
- Obedience reveals true allegiance.  
- Freedom in Christ calls for intentional submission to righteousness.  
- Repeated choices shape spiritual direction over time.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Joshua 24:15:** “Choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve…”  
- **John 14:15:** “If you love Me, keep My commands.”  
- **Galatians 5:16:** “Walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh.”  

Examine your direction today. Choose obedience that reflects trust in God, and allow your daily decisions to align your heart more fully with His righteousness and life.
