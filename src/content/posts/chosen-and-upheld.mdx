# Chosen and Upheld  
**Date:** February 28, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Isaiah 42:1

> ““Look at my servant, whom I strengthen. He is my chosen one, who pleases me. I have put my Spirit upon him. He will bring justice to the nations.”

Isaiah introduces a powerful image of God’s chosen Servant—upheld, delighted in, and empowered by the Spirit. This prophecy ultimately points to Christ, revealing the heart of God in action. The Servant is not self-appointed or self-sustained. He is chosen and strengthened by the Father.

This verse reminds us that calling begins with God. What He initiates, He also supports. The Servant’s mission flows from divine appointment, not personal ambition. God’s Spirit equips and sustains the work He sets in motion.

Isaiah 42:1 also reveals God’s pleasure in obedience. The Father delights in the faithful Servant who walks in alignment with His will. This delight reflects relationship, not mere performance. When obedience flows from trust, it becomes a source of joy in God’s sight.

As we conclude this month, Isaiah 42:1 invites reflection on calling and dependence. Are we striving in our own strength, or resting in the One who upholds us? God sustains what He ordains, and His Spirit empowers what He appoints.

## Main Takeaways  
- Calling originates with God, not personal ambition.  
- God upholds and sustains those He appoints.  
- Obedience aligned with God’s will brings Him delight.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Matthew 3:17:** “This is My Son, whom I love; with Him I am well pleased.”  
- **Philippians 2:13:** “For it is God who works in you…”  
- **Hebrews 1:3:** “Sustaining all things by His powerful word.”  

Rest in God’s sustaining power today. Trust that if He has called you, He will uphold you, and walk forward confident that His Spirit equips you for every purpose He places before you.
