# Do Not Boast About Tomorrow  
**Date:** March 5, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Proverbs 27:1

> “Don’t brag about tomorrow, since you don’t know what the day will bring.”

Proverbs offers a simple but powerful reminder about the uncertainty of life. People often speak confidently about what they will accomplish tomorrow, assuming the future is firmly within their control. Yet this verse challenges that mindset by reminding us that no one truly knows what a day may bring.

This wisdom is not meant to discourage planning, but to encourage humility. Life can change quickly. Opportunities appear and disappear, circumstances shift, and events unfold in ways we cannot predict. Recognizing this truth helps us approach the future with a posture of dependence on God rather than confidence in our own control.

Proverbs 27:1 also encourages us to value the present. When we assume tomorrow is guaranteed, we can easily overlook the significance of today. Gratitude, obedience, and meaningful relationships often grow when we recognize the gift of the moment we are living in right now.

As we move through today, this verse invites reflection on how we view the future. Instead of boasting about tomorrow, we are called to trust the One who holds it. Faith allows us to plan wisely while remaining humble about what lies ahead.

## Main Takeaways  
- The future is uncertain and ultimately outside our control.  
- Humility grows when we recognize our dependence on God.  
- Today is a gift that should not be overlooked.

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **James 4:13-15:** “You do not even know what will happen tomorrow…”  
- **Psalm 90:12:** “Teach us to number our days…”

Live today with gratitude and humility. Trust God with tomorrow while making the most of the opportunities and responsibilities placed before you right now.
