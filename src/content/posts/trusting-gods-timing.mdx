# Trusting in God’s Timing

**Date:** August 30, 2024 

**Scripture Reading:** Ecclesiastes 3:1

> “To everything there is a season, a time for every purpose under heaven.”

Waiting is one of the hardest parts of the Christian walk. We want answers, movement, and clarity — but God often calls us to wait with trust.

Ecclesiastes 3:1 reminds us that God has purpose in every season. His timing is not delayed; it is perfect. When we feel stuck or uncertain, we remember that He sees what we cannot.

Abraham and Sarah waited years for God’s promise, but His timing brought greater glory and deeper faith.

## Main Takeaways

- Every season has purpose.
- Waiting strengthens our faith.
- God is always faithful, even when timing feels unclear.

## Cross-References

- Romans 8:28
- Psalm 27:14
- Isaiah 40:31

**Closing Thought:**  
Trust His timing. What He has promised, He will fulfill in due season.
