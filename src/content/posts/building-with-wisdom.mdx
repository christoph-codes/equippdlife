# Building With Wisdom  
**Date:** December 11, 2025 

**Scripture Reading:** Proverbs 24:3-4

> *“By wisdom a house is built,  
> and through understanding it is established;  
> through knowledge its rooms are filled  
> with rare and beautiful treasures.”*

Proverbs reminds us that building something meaningful, whether it’s a home, a family, a ministry, or a life of faith, requires more than hard work. It requires wisdom, understanding, and knowledge that come from God. These verses point us to a deeper truth: lasting strength is not created by human effort alone, but by a heart aligned with God’s ways.

“By wisdom a house is built.” Wisdom is the foundation. It shapes our decisions, guides our steps, and keeps us from building on unstable ground. When God’s wisdom leads us, what we build can stand.

“Through understanding it is established.” Understanding gives depth. It brings clarity about God’s character, His Word, and His purposes. It roots us so that what we build is not easily shaken by circumstances or emotions.

“Through knowledge its rooms are filled.” This is the overflow, the fruit of a life shaped by God. Knowledge of Him fills every part of our lives with beauty, blessing, and richness that cannot be manufactured by human strength. These “treasures” are things like peace, integrity, joy, faithfulness, and spiritual maturity.

## Main Takeaways  
- Lasting strength comes from building life on God’s wisdom.  
- Understanding deepens and stabilizes what God is forming in us.  
- Knowledge of God fills every area of life with beauty and spiritual treasure.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Proverbs 3:5-6:** “Trust in the Lord with all your heart…”  
- **James 1:5:** “If any of you lacks wisdom, ask God…”  
- **Colossians 2:3:** “In Christ are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.”  

Ask God to build your life with His wisdom today. Invite Him to guide your decisions, strengthen what He is establishing in you, and fill every room of your life with the treasures that only come from knowing Him.
