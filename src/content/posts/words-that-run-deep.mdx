# Words That Run Deep  
**Date:** February 1, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** Proverbs 18:4

> “Wise words are like deep waters; wisdom flows from the wise like a bubbling brook.”

Proverbs 18:4 points us to the power and depth of words shaped by wisdom. Not every word spoken carries weight, but words rooted in understanding have the ability to reach places that surface-level talk cannot. Wisdom-filled speech does not rush, impress, or dominate. It flows with purpose and brings clarity where there is confusion.

This verse invites us to consider the source of our words. Shallow speech often reflects hurried thoughts or unexamined hearts. In contrast, words that refresh others are drawn from a heart shaped by God’s truth. Wisdom does not simply inform what we say; it influences how and when we speak.

The imagery of flowing water reminds us that wise words bring life. Just as water sustains and renews, speech guided by wisdom encourages, restores, and strengthens. These words do not manipulate or tear down. They build trust and point others toward truth.

As we step into this new month, Proverbs 18:4 invites reflection on how we communicate. Our words have the potential to either drain or refresh those around us. When we allow God to shape our hearts, our words begin to carry depth that blesses others and honors Him.

## Main Takeaways  
- Words rooted in wisdom carry depth and influence.  
- Wise speech flows from a heart shaped by God’s truth.  
- Our words can refresh and bring life to others.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Proverbs 10:11:** “The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life.”  
- **James 3:10-11:** “Out of the same mouth come praise and cursing…”  
- **Colossians 4:6:** “Let your conversation be always full of grace…”  

As you speak today, slow down and draw from wisdom rather than impulse. Ask God to shape your heart so that your words flow with life, clarity, and grace, refreshing everyone they reach.
