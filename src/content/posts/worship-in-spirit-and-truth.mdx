# Worship in Spirit and Truth  
**Date:** February 2, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** John 4:23

> “But the time is coming—indeed it’s here now—when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth. The Father is looking for those who will worship him that way.”

Jesus shifts the conversation about worship away from location and tradition and toward the condition of the heart. He explains that true worship is not bound to a mountain, a temple, or a specific form. What God seeks is worship that is genuine, sincere, and grounded in truth.

Worship in spirit speaks to authenticity. It is worship that flows from the inner life, not outward performance. It reflects a heart fully engaged with God, responsive to His presence, and open to His leading. Worship in truth anchors that sincerity in who God truly is. It is shaped by His Word, His character, and His revealed will, rather than personal preference or convenience.

This verse reminds us that worship is not limited to songs or gatherings. It extends into daily life, shaping how we live, serve, and respond to God. When spirit and truth come together, worship becomes a way of life rather than a moment on a schedule.

As we continue forward, John 4:23 invites us to reflect on how we worship. Are we engaging God with honesty and reverence? True worship honors God not by form alone, but by hearts aligned with His truth and surrendered to His presence.

## Main Takeaways  
- God seeks worship that is sincere and rooted in truth.  
- True worship flows from the heart, not just outward form.  
- Worship extends beyond gatherings into daily life.  

## Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Psalm 51:17:** “A broken and contrite heart You, God, will not despise.”  
- **Romans 12:1:** “Offer your bodies as a living sacrifice…”  
- **Philippians 3:3:** “We worship by the Spirit of God…”  

Approach God today with sincerity and humility. Let your worship be shaped by His truth and fueled by a heart fully devoted to Him, knowing that this is the worship He seeks and delights in.
