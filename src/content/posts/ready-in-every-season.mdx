# Ready in Every Season  
**Date:** February 5, 2026

**Scripture Reading:** 2 Timothy 4:2

> “Preach the word of God. Be prepared, whether the time is favorable or not. Patiently correct, rebuke, and encourage your people with good teaching.”

Paul’s instruction to Timothy carries urgency and weight. He calls him to remain ready at all times, not only when conditions are favorable. Faithfulness to God’s Word is not seasonal. It is lived out consistently, whether the moment feels convenient or challenging.

This verse speaks to perseverance. Being ready requires attentiveness, courage, and commitment. Paul does not instruct Timothy to wait for ideal circumstances before speaking truth. Instead, he emphasizes obedience rooted in conviction. God’s truth does not change based on culture, comfort, or timing.

Readiness also involves patience and love. Paul pairs boldness with gentleness, reminding Timothy that correction and encouragement must be guided by care. Truth spoken without love can wound, while love without truth can mislead. God calls His people to hold both faithfully.

As we move through this season, 2 Timothy 4:2 invites us to examine our willingness to remain faithful. Are we prepared to stand firm when obedience feels costly? God honors those who remain steady, trusting Him through every season.

### Main Takeaways  
- Faithfulness to God’s Word is not dependent on circumstances.  
- Readiness requires courage, patience, and commitment.  
- Truth must be spoken with both boldness and love.  

### Cross-Referencing Scriptures  
- **Colossians 4:6:** “Let your conversation be always full of grace…”  
- **1 Peter 3:15:** “Always be prepared to give an answer…”  
- **Galatians 6:9:** “Let us not become weary in doing good…”  

Commit today to remain faithful in every season. Stay grounded in God’s Word, speak truth with love, and trust that obedience honors God regardless of the circumstances you face.
